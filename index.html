LexiBear 小熊单词本 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 6 日　|　最后更新：2026 年 10 月 6 日

一句话说明：你的单词和复习记录只存在你自己的手机里，我们不上传、不收集、不查看。
关于广告：本应用免费提供全部功能，界面底部会展示横幅广告，广告由 Google AdMob 提供。为此 Google 会读取设备上的广告标识符等技术信息，详见第四节。

引言

LexiBear 小熊单词本（以下简称「本应用」）是一款英文单词学习工具，由开发者个人独立开发并发布。我们非常重视你的隐私，本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

一、我们收集什么、不收集什么

本应用没有账号系统，不需要注册或登录，我们本身不收集、不存储、不传输以下信息：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话号码等个人身份信息
  • 位置信息
  • 通讯录、短信、通话记录
  • 照片、相机、麦克风内容
  • 你添加的单词、复习进度等学习记录

需要如实说明的是：为了在你的设备上展示广告，本应用集成的第三方广告 SDK（Google AdMob）会读取设备上的广告标识符等技术信息。这部分数据由 Google 处理、不由我们控制，详见第四节。除此之外，本应用不含统计/分析 SDK、不含崩溃上报 SDK、不含推送 SDK。

二、你的数据保存在哪里

你的全部单词和复习进度，只保存在你手机本机的应用存储空间内（浏览器 localStorage），不发送到我们的服务器，也不上传到任何云端。

这意味着：

  • 我们看不到你添加了哪些单词，也无法恢复你丢失的数据；
  • 卸载应用或清除应用数据会导致记录丢失，请定期使用应用内的备份功能；
  • 你在「设置 → 数据管理」中删除，数据立即从本机移除，无需向我们申请。

由于数据从未离开你的设备，不存在「数据保留期限」问题，也没有需要删除的服务器副本。

三、联网时会发生什么

本应用的核心功能完全支持离线使用（内置约 4.5 万词的离线词库）。只有当你主动查询一个单词且设备处于联网状态时，应用才会把该单词本身发送给以下第三方服务：

服务用途发送内容
免费在线词典服务（dictionaryapi.dev）补全英文释义、同义词、反义词你查询的单词
AI 大模型服务生成中文释义、例句翻译、引申用法与词源趣闻你查询的单词及固定提示词
图片生成服务（pollinations.ai）为单词生成配图你查询的单词
  • 只发送单词本身，不附带账号、设备 ID、Cookie 或安装标识，无法关联回具体的某个人；
  • 这些服务仅在处理当次请求期间于内存中使用，请求完成即结束，不用于建立广告画像或用户画像，也不被我们留存或汇总；
  • 应用不含自身的服务器，我们不会把上述数据二次转存或转卖；
  • 所有网络通信均采用 HTTPS 加密传输；
  • 断网状态下应用不发起任何网络请求，全部功能回退到本机离线词库，广告也不会显示。

四、广告与广告 SDK（Google AdMob）

4.1 应用里有广告

本应用免费提供全部功能，通过在界面底部展示横幅广告来维持运营。广告由 Google AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）提供。我们自身不销售广告位、不接触广告主，也无法看到你的任何个人信息。

4.2 广告 SDK 可能收集的信息

为了投放广告、衡量广告效果并防止无效流量，Google AdMob SDK 可能收集并使用：

类型具体内容用途
设备或其他 ID广告标识符（Android 广告 ID / AAID）、应用集 ID（App Set ID）广告投放与频次控制、广告效果衡量、防欺诈
粗略技术信息IP 地址（由此可推断大致地区）、设备型号、系统版本、语言广告投放、合规与风控
广告互动广告的展示与点击情况广告效果衡量、防欺诈

这些数据不会与你的学习记录关联——你的单词和复习进度始终只在本机、从不上传，广告 SDK 也无法访问它们。

4.3 数据如何被共享

上述数据会通过 HTTPS 加密传输给 Google 及其广告合作伙伴，用于广告投放与效果衡量。它们的数据处理受 Google 自己的隐私政策约束：

  • Google 隐私政策：policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google 如何使用数据：policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google 广告技术说明：policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4.4 你的选择

  • 重置或删除广告 ID：Android「设置 → 隐私 → 广告」中可随时重置，或直接删除该标识；
  • 关闭个性化广告：在同一位置选择退出后，你仍会看到广告，但内容与你的兴趣无关；
  • 欧洲经济区（EEA）与英国用户：首次启动时本应用会通过 Google 官方同意管理平台（UMP）征求你的广告数据使用同意。拒绝同意不影响任何学习功能，只是广告会以非个性化方式投放；
  • 不想要广告：卸载应用即可，本应用不会因为未观看广告而限制任何功能。

4.5 我们为什么选择 AdMob

AdMob 是 Google 官方的移动广告平台，本身须遵守 Google Play「广告」政策与相关数据政策。我们没有接入任何其他广告联盟、归因平台或数据中介。

五、备份文件

应用提供以下备份方式，全部由你主动触发：

  • 备份到本机：在你手机「下载/LexiBear」文件夹生成 words.csv 文件，保存在你的设备上，我们不接触；
  • 备份到邮箱：通过 Android 系统分享机制唤起你自己设备上的邮件应用并预填收件人、主题与附件，邮件由你本人确认并发送；
  • 导入备份：从你选择的文件中恢复数据。

我们不会读取、上传或留存你的备份文件。

六、应用申请的权限

权限用途
网络访问（INTERNET）联网补全单词资料（在线词典 / AI 讲解 / 配图）以及加载广告
写入外部存储（仅 Android 9 及以下）将备份文件 words.csv 保存到手机「下载/LexiBear」文件夹

我们不申请位置、相机、麦克风、通讯录、短信、电话、日历、健康数据等任何敏感权限，也不申请全文件访问权限。

七、儿童隐私

本应用面向 13 岁及以上的英语学习者，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集儿童的任何个人信息。应用内的广告同样不会针对 13 岁以下儿童进行个性化投放；如果你未满 13 岁，请勿使用本应用。如你发现在未经同意的情况下涉及儿童信息，请联系我们处理。

八、数据安全

  • 你的学习记录保存在本机应用沙盒内，其他应用无法访问；
  • 所有网络传输（包括学习资料请求与广告请求）均使用 HTTPS 加密；
  • 由于我们本身不存储你的数据，不存在因我们的服务器被攻击而导致你数据泄露的风险。

九、本政策的变更

如本政策发生重大变更，我们会更新本页顶部的「最后更新」日期，并在应用新版本发布时同步告知。建议你定期查阅。

LexiBear · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026  |  Last updated: October 6, 2026

In short: your words and review progress stay on your own device. We do not upload, collect, or view them.
About ads: the App is free in full and displays a banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. For this, Google reads technical identifiers such as your advertising ID — see Section 4.

Introduction

LexiBear ("the App") is an English vocabulary learning tool developed and published by an individual developer. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

1. What We Do and Do Not Collect

The App has no account system and requires no sign-up or login. We ourselves do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identifiers such as name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • Photos, camera, or microphone content
  • Your saved words and review progress

To be transparent: to display ads on your device, the third-party advertising SDK (Google AdMob) integrated into the App reads technical identifiers such as the advertising ID. That data is processed by Google, not by us — see Section 4. Apart from that, the App contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or push-notification SDKs.

2. Where Your Data Lives

All of your words and review progress are stored only in local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to our servers or to any cloud service. We cannot see which words you add, and we cannot recover data you lose. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data will remove your records — please use the built-in backup feature regularly.

Because your data never leaves your device, there is no server-side retention period and no server copy to delete.

3. What Happens When You Are Online

The App works fully offline with a built-in ~45,000-word dictionary. Only when you actively look up a word while online does the App send the word itself to the following third-party services:

ServicePurposeData sent
Free dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev)English definitions, synonyms, antonymsThe word you look up
AI language serviceChinese meanings, example translations, etymology notesThe word and a fixed prompt
Image generation service (pollinations.ai)Illustrative image for the wordThe word you look up
  • Only the word itself is sent. No account, device ID, cookie, or install identifier is attached, so the data cannot be linked back to any individual.
  • These services use the data only in memory while serving that single request. It is not used to build advertising or user profiles, and it is not retained or aggregated by us.
  • The App has no backend server of its own; we do not re-store or resell this data.
  • All network traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Offline, the App makes no network requests at all and falls back entirely to the local dictionary. Ads are not displayed, since they cannot be loaded without a connection.

4. Advertising and the Ad SDK (Google AdMob)

4.1 The App contains ads

The App is free to use in full and is supported by a banner ad shown at the bottom of the screen. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). We do not sell ad space, deal with advertisers, or have access to any of your personal information.

4.2 What the ad SDK may collect

To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google AdMob SDK may collect and use:

TypeDetailsPurpose
Device or other IDsAdvertising ID (AAID), App Set IDAd delivery and frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention
Coarse technical dataIP address (approximate region), device model, OS version, languageAd delivery, compliance, fraud prevention
Ad interactionsImpressions and clicksAd measurement, fraud prevention

This data is never linked to your learning records — your words and progress stay on your device and are never uploaded, and the ad SDK cannot access them.

4.3 How the data is shared

This data is transmitted over HTTPS to Google and its advertising partners for ad delivery and measurement. Their processing is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google ad technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4.4 Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: available any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising: the same setting lets you opt out; you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • EEA and UK users: on first launch the App asks for your consent to ad data usage through Google's official consent management platform (UMP). Declining does not limit any learning feature — ads are simply served non-personalised.
  • Prefer no ads at all: simply uninstall the App. No feature is ever gated behind watching an ad.

4.5 Why AdMob

AdMob is Google's own mobile advertising platform and is subject to Google Play's Ads policy and associated data policies. We have integrated no other ad network, attribution platform, or data broker.

5. Backup Files

All backup actions are initiated by you:

  • Back up to this device — creates a words.csv file in your Downloads/LexiBear folder. The file stays on your device; we never access it.
  • Back up to email — the App uses the standard Android share mechanism to open your own email app with recipient, subject, and attachment pre-filled. You confirm and send it.
  • Import backup — restores data from a file you select.

We do not read, upload, or retain your backup files.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for English learners aged 13 and above and is not directed to children under 13. Ads in the App are likewise not personalised for children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not use the App. If you believe a child's information has been involved without consent, please contact us.

8. Data Security

  • Your learning records live in the App's private sandbox, inaccessible to other apps.
  • All network transmission — including learning-material requests and ad requests — uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Because we do not store your data, there is no server-side breach risk of ours affecting you.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date above and inform you with a new App release. Please review it periodically.
